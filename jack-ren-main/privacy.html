How We Protect Your Information

Last updated: 2026-01-06

Overview

Jack Travels values the confidentiality of every traveler and partner. We request only the details needed to arrange trips and fulfill requested services.

Details We Collect

  • Journey information: Preferences and traveler details used to prepare routes and handle reservations.
  • Inquiries: Information sent through our inquiry form so we can answer questions and organize travel plans.

Site Usage Insights

We use narrow website analytics to assess performance and make navigation easier. Personal information remains protected, and we collect only details that serve a clear purpose.

Available Controls

  • Talk to Us us to review or request removal of personal information connected with a booking or inquiry.
  • Select the unsubscribe link in our messages if you prefer to stop receiving travel updates.

Talk to Us Us

For questions about data or privacy, complete the contact form on our homepage or connect with customer support.
